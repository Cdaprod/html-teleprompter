Whats a pretextual detainment? 

Imagine the police show up for a noise complaint, but the party's already over... Using that "resolved issue" as a legal pretext, with the true motive of digging for evidence of a bigger crime, one they don't actually have the legal cause to investigate you for.

A pretextual stop is an act of retaliation. It is the moment an officer, lacking any valid reason to suspect you of a serious crime, chooses to use the full weight of their authority against you anyway. They find a pretext--a noise complaint, a lane change--and use it as a legal shield to detain, intimidate, and investigate. Your fear becomes their probable cause. Your past trauma becomes their present excuse. It transforms a peace officer into an aggressor, and your rights into negotiable suggestions.
