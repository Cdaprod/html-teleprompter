to not walk away.

waking up screaming,

isn’t unusual.

The cost of becoming a man
capable of carrying
the life I have now.

SECTION D -- THE COST OF BEING GOOD
------------------------------------

Here’s the part I wish someone had told me earlier:

Being good is not a feeling.
It’s not clarity.
It’s not motivation.

It’s behavior.

Boring behavior.
Unrewarded behavior.
Behavior that doesn’t trend, doesn’t scale, and doesn’t look impressive from the outside.

It’s doing the thing you said you’d do… when no one is watching.
It’s apologizing without explaining.
It’s being consistent when inconsistency would be easier.

[pause]

Goodness isn’t intensity.
It’s reliability.

It’s your name meaning the same thing in every room.

------------------------------------
SECTION E -- THE DARK SIDE, ACCURATELY DESCRIBED
------------------------------------

When I say I learned about the darkest side of being human,
I don’t mean crime.
Or addiction.
Or violence.

I mean the internal architecture that allows those things to exist.

Entitlement disguised as pain.
Self-pity with a legal argument.
The belief that rules are for everyone else… because your suffering is "different."

That’s the real darkness.
Not what we do.
But how convincing we become to ourselves while doing it.

------------------------------------
SECTION F -- RETURN TO THE PRESENT
------------------------------------

Now, years later, the noise is different.

It’s batteries dying.
Arguments over who gets to chill in the closet.
Someone correcting me with my full government name.

And that noise… that ordinary, lovely, beautiful sounding noise…
is the sound of accountability working.

[pause]

The man in that car needed quiet.
He was wrong.

He needed something louder than his excuses.

------------------------------------
CLOSING THOUGHT -- OPEN LOOP
------------------------------------

If there’s anything I want to leave you with, it’s this:

There's no wall that is "the end".
It’s the audit.

It’s the moment where the cost of staying finally outweighs the fear of leaving.

And if you’re sitting in your own version of that silence right now…
listening to the math repeat itself…

Just know this:
Walking away is for the best.

It’s the first honest thing you could do for yourself,
in a long time.
