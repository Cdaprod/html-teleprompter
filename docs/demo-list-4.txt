WHEN THE RECORD LEAVES A BOOTPRINT

Every cop is an expert at taking away rights.

────────────────────────

Pretext is a funny thing.

It doesn’t start loud. It starts small.

A look. A pause. A moment that feels like nothing.

Somewhere between voluntary
and not free to leave,
something changes.

Not because a crime happened.

Because compliance was expected.

────────────────────────

You’re going to see my body react.

It isn’t fear.
It’s recognition.

My body remembers moments
where compliance didn’t end the encounter.

While my mind stays calm
because it knows what matters now:
tone,
timing,
and not giving the moment anything extra.

────────────────────────

I pulled my public record
because memory lies.

Paper doesn’t.

What came back was heavy.

Outcomes were there.
Charges.
Convictions.

What wasn’t there
was how anything started.

No stops.
No explanations.

Just results.

────────────────────────

Thirteen years ago,
I didn’t question this.

I thought authority worked like gravity.

You don’t argue with it.
You brace.

────────────────────────

I wasn’t innocent.

That matters.

But innocence isn’t the standard.

Lawful is.

────────────────────────

The past doesn’t stay in the past.

It waits.

In databases.
In assumptions.
In half-written records.

Every time my name gets run,
something old wakes up.

────────────────────────

Patterns don’t announce themselves.

They repeat
until they feel normal.

That’s how they hide.

────────────────────────

People ask me one question.

If you did nothing wrong,
why does it matter?

Because that assumes
rights are rewards.

They aren’t.

────────────────────────

Authority doesn’t disappear
when you comply.

It stays.

In records.
In future encounters.

The moment refusal becomes justification,
voluntary ends.

────────────────────────

I’m not documenting police.

I’m documenting process.

Because process is where power lives.

────────────────────────

If nothing happened,
the record should show that.

And if something did --

it should leave
the smallest footprint possible.
